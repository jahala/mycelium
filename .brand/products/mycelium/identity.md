## Positioning

Peer messaging for agents. umbel fans workers out from one stem and pleach conducts a plan
through gates; both speak down the tree. mycelium is the network beneath: one agent asks
another and uses the answer, without a supervisor relaying it. A stranger knocks, and a person
decides who is let in.

## Mark

Two of the same mushroom, joined underground by one thread, in ink on a tile of mycelium's bloom
(`#C9A18D`). The owner asked on 2026-10-27 for mushrooms only, one colour and the same shape:
the mushrooms are equal peers, and the thread is the network beneath them, where what passes between
them travels. Filled caps and a stroked thread keep the garden's diagram language; the shape sits
apart from tilth's seedling over a ground line and a root tree.
